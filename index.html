Important Limitations

  • Gridded climate data may differ from actual farm measurements.
  • Soil moisture estimates are not a replacement for field sampling.
  • Rainfall is not the same as irrigation water availability.
  • Climate screening alone cannot determine crop suitability.
  • Crop rotation scenarios are examples, not certified recommendations.
  • GAEZ results depend on the dataset and its units and classification.
