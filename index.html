4. Crop Recommendations

Compare illustrative crop profiles with the available climate and farm information. Load climate data and complete the farm form before generating results.

The scores are preliminary screening indicators based on simplified crop profiles. They are not validated agronomic recommendations. Local growing calendars, soil tests, crop varieties and farming advice are still required.

5. Crop Rotation Scenarios

Explore possible crop sequences. Legumes may contribute to nitrogen cycling when properly managed, but results depend on species, soil and management.

Data Sources and Limitations

NASA POWER provides climate information. This prototype currently uses daily temperature and precipitation data. Other satellite products, field measurements and local agricultural information have not yet been integrated.

Satellite and climate data have different spatial resolutions and time periods. The tool should be used for exploration and decision support, not as a substitute for soil testing or professional agricultural advice.
