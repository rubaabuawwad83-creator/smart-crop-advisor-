4. Crop Climate Comparison

Review broad climate ranges and farm conditions for selected crops. This section does not rank crops or generate validated suitability scores.

Crop requirements vary by variety, planting date, soil depth, drainage, elevation and management. Climate ranges shown here are broad screening references only, not locally validated thresholds or planting advice.

5. Crop Rotation Scenarios

Explore example crop sequences and consider the previous crop when planning a rotation.

Rotation planning should consider botanical family, local pests and diseases, herbicide carryover, nutrient needs, water availability and market conditions. A legume does not guarantee a specific increase in soil nitrogen.

Data Sources and Limitations

  • NASA POWER: daily temperature, precipitation and relative humidity data.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles displayed through Leaflet.
  • Soil moisture: only available when a CSV file is supplied by the user.
  • GAEZ: only available when a source data file is supplied by the user.
NASA POWER climate data are gridded estimates and do not represent direct measurements at the farm. RH2M is relative humidity at 2 metres, not soil moisture. PRECTOTCORR is precipitation, not irrigation or water availability. This tool is an exploratory decision-support application, not a certified agronomic service.
