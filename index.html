9. Data Sources and Limitations

NASA POWER

Daily temperature, precipitation, and relative humidity for agroclimatology.

NASA POWER Portal
Open-Meteo Historical Weather API & ERA5

Historical climate and volumetric soil moisture estimates.

Open-Meteo Documentation
NASA GIBS (MODIS Terra CorrectedReflectance TrueColor)

Satellite imagery map layer for visual geographic context.

NASA GIBS Portal
FAO GAEZ

Global agro-ecological zoning and crop suitability dataset import support.

FAO GAEZ Portal

Limitations

  • Gridded data represents regional meteorological models, not direct sensor readings at field-scale.
  • Soil moisture outputs are modeled estimates and do not substitute for on-site physical soil testing.
  • Crop comparison results are screening indicators, not absolute yield guarantees.
